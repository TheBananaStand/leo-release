This Mac becomes Leo’s link to Apple services.

Leo runs on a Linux server on your home network — always on, always fast. The Mac app is the bridge: iMessage in, Calendar and Reminders synced, iOS control in your pocket.

Getting started

Up and running in 15 minutes

1

Download Leo for Mac

Install the app above. It handles Apple ecosystem access and guides you through the rest of setup.

2

Flash Leo OS to a USB drive

Download the Leo OS installer from Releases and flash it to a USB drive (8 GB+). Any spare x86_64 PC works as the server.

3

Boot your home server

Boot from USB and follow the on-screen installer. Leo OS sets everything up automatically.

4

Leo finds itself

The Mac app discovers Leo on your local network automatically and walks you through pairing. No port forwarding, no config files.
